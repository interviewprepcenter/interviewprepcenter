Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

InterviewPrepCenter collects information necessary to provide the personalized interview-preparation service, including name, email, WhatsApp/mobile number, target company, target job title, professional experience, resume, target job description and interview date where provided.

Resume and job-description information is used to prepare the customer's personalized materials and provide related customer service.

Customer information will not be sold to third parties.

Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. InterviewPrepCenter does not directly collect or store customers' card, banking or payment credentials.

Uploaded documents and related customer information are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, complete delivery, resolve support requests and meet applicable legal or business record-keeping requirements.

Customers should not upload passwords, financial information, confidential company credentials or unrelated sensitive information.
