GOOD TO KNOW

Before you purchase.

What do I need to provide?

You provide your resume, target job description and the requested interview details after payment.

How many interview questions are included?

The preparation pack includes 30 personalized interview questions with detailed answer guidance and follow-up preparation.

Will the answers claim experience I do not have?

The preparation is designed to distinguish documented experience from transferable or conceptual knowledge. It should not be used to claim hands-on experience that your profile does not support.

Is this limited to engineering or SRE roles?

No. The website is intentionally role-agnostic. The preparation is driven by the resume and target JD rather than by a fixed job title.

Is this a guarantee of interview success?

No. This is an interview-preparation service. It does not guarantee interview selection, employment, promotion, compensation or any specific interview outcome.
